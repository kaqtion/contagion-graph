Every headline has a blast radius.

“Plain RAG retrieves documents; Contagion Graph retrieves consequences.”

Corporate risk does not stop at direct tier-1 counterparties. When a major earthquake halts semiconductor foundries in Taiwan, or trade sanctions restrict lithography tooling, the decisive damage cascades through 2nd- and 3rd-hop dependencies: substrate suppliers, packaging subcontractors, automotive MCU makers, and consumer hardware integrators across multiple jurisdictions.

• LLMs Hallucinate Counterparties
General generative models invent plausible-sounding suppliers from training co-occurrence. They cannot verify operational contracts or calculate auditable financial impact weights.
• SEC 10-K Filings Are Opaque
Firms bury sole-supplier risks and >10% customer revenue thresholds across thousands of legal pages. Standard keyword RAG retrieves isolated sentences but fails on transitive multi-hop chains.

03 Mathematical Scoring Formulation

Impact(c) = S_e × polarity × ∑ [ ∏ (w_uv · s_uv) ] × λ^(hops - 1) × f_fresh(p)
S_e: Severity (0.2–1.0) • polarity: ±1 • w_uv: 10-K dependency weight • s_uv: +1 customer / -1 competitor • λ: Hop decay factor (0.50) • f_fresh: Evidence freshness decay (>12mo)
